Weixiang Guo / 郭韦翔

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science (Engineering) in Electronics Technology at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. I conduct research at the NTU Internet of Things Lab and CARTIN, advised by Prof. Lihua Xie and Dr. Rui Jin.

My research is united by a common goal: understanding how agents perceive, reason, plan, and interact in complex real-world environments. This includes both embodied agents that integrate multimodal perception, decision-making, and action and autonomous robotic systems that operate reliably under real-world constraints.

I currently focus on integrating vision-language-action (VLA) models with aerial manipulation, exploring how aerial robots can interpret tasks from visual observations and language instructions, and coordinate motion planning and manipulation control to perform multi-step physical interaction tasks in real-world environments.

中文简介

目前，我在新加坡南洋理工大学电气与电子工程学院攻读电子技术专业工程硕士（Master of Science (Engineering) in Electronics Technology），并在 NTU Internet of Things Lab 与先进机器人技术创新中心（CARTIN）开展研究，指导老师为谢立华教授与金锐博士。

我的研究围绕一个共同目标展开：理解智能体如何在复杂的真实环境中进行感知、推理、规划与交互。这既包括将多模态感知、决策与行动相结合的具身智能体，也包括能够在现实约束下可靠运行的自主机器人系统。

目前，我主要聚焦于视觉—语言—动作（VLA）模型与空中操作的结合，探索如何让空中机器人根据视觉观测和语言指令理解任务，并通过协调运动规划与操作控制，在真实环境中完成连续、多步骤的物理交互任务。
