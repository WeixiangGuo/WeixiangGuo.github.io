Weixiang Guo / 郭韦翔

Hi! I am Weixiang Guo (郭韦翔), currently pursuing an M.Sc. (Eng.) at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. I am also a research student with the NTU Internet of Things Lab and the Centre for Advanced Robotics Technology Innovation (CARTIN), under the supervision of Prof. Lihua Xie and Dr. Rui Jin.

My research interests lie in robotics and autonomous systems, with a focus on robot perception and planning, embodied intelligence, and vision-language-action models. I am particularly interested in connecting multimodal perception with robust decision-making and motion planning, and validating these methods on real robotic platforms.

Before joining NTU, I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from Taiyuan University of Technology. My background spans algorithms, embedded systems, and hardware prototyping, and I enjoy turning research ideas into reliable robotic systems that work in real-world environments.

中文简介

你好！我是郭韦翔，目前在新加坡南洋理工大学电气与电子工程学院攻读工程硕士学位，同时在 NTU Internet of Things Lab 与先进机器人技术创新中心（CARTIN）开展研究工作，指导老师为谢立华教授与金锐博士。

我的研究方向主要集中在机器人与自主系统，包括机器人感知与规划、具身智能以及视觉—语言—动作模型。我尤其关注如何将多模态环境感知与稳健的决策、运动规划相结合，并在真实机器人平台上完成系统实现与实验验证。

本科期间，我就读于太原理工大学电子信息工程专业。我的技术背景涵盖算法开发、嵌入式系统与硬件原型设计，也希望将研究构想转化为能够在真实环境中稳定运行的机器人系统。
